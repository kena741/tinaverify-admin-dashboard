# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Platform operators (superusers and permissioned staff) who manage Tina Verify businesses, billing, and support from a web admin.

## Product Purpose

Tina Verify admin lets Zulu staff oversee restaurant/business accounts: owners, subscriptions, payments, platform roles, and operational tools. Success is fast, trustworthy ops — find a business, act, and leave a clear trail.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, operational. Dense when needed, never decorative for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboards (purple gradients, glow, ornamental cards), marketing-landing layouts inside the admin shell, inventing nonstandard controls for standard filter/table tasks.

## Design Principles

1. Familiar admin patterns over novelty — tables, filters, and sheets should feel like Linear/Stripe ops tools.
2. Context before action — prefer business/owner detail over orphaned global tools.
3. Show trust signals — loading, empty, and error states that teach the next step.
4. Density with clarity — information-rich lists; labels stay readable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, WCAG AA contrast for body and form text.
